Drooping-ear attachment correction, 2026-10-20

Reported screenshots identify ears_22 (말랑 강아지) and ears_30 (코코 스패니얼).
These sprites attach at their upper inner openings, whereas their original metadata
used a bottom-center root and the generic 0.36-height sink. That hid their hanging
lower sections and exposed disconnected ink ends above the head.

Final correction uses each real inner attachment root, sink 0, a fixed ear rotation,
and a small inward/downward offset. All corrected ears remain behind the body (z10).
This hides the intentionally open root inside the head without hiding the dangling
lower ear. Original images, outline style, design size, and pink interiors are kept.

Corrected IDs: ears_07, ears_08, ears_22, ears_30, ears_33.
The first two are legacy ears with the same upper-inner attachment structure.
ears_27 and ears_34 have genuine lower roots and keep their original metadata.
