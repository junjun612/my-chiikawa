Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a transparent 4 by 4 sprite sheet of small, simple accessories for the existing Chiikawa-like hand-drawn character maker. This is a conservative style repair, not a new character style.

Reference image 1 is the visual style target: warm dark-brown narrow rounded hand-drawn contours, very simple flat milky pastel colors, understated tiny accessories, friendly curves and plenty of quiet white space. Reference image 2 is an existing game ribbon for basic detachable-asset construction only. Reference image 3 is an existing small tail for attachment orientation only. Keep the style closer to the first reference; do not thicken the outline into a big black sticker border. No painterly shading, 3D, glossy material or heavy texture. No character heads, bodies, faces, eyes, ears or whole characters.

Composition: square image with exactly 4 equal columns and 4 equal rows. Exactly one complete accessory asset in each cell. A paired-wing asset contains two mirrored detached wings, both fully inside that cell with a clear small transparent gap between them. Each complete asset occupies 55 to 65 percent of its cell; all contours stay well inside the cell with generous transparent gutters. A genuine transparent alpha background everywhere outside each accessory. No grid lines, labels, text, watermarks, decorative scatter or cast shadows.

Row 1: Four variants of a tiny ivory or cream ribbon hairclip, left to right: (1) very small rounded ivory bow, two soft loops, short stubby tails, pale blush center knot; (2) small pale peach two-loop bow with cream knot and no tails; (3) a tiny ivory bow with two softly folded loops and two short curved tails, one light lavender knot; (4) tiny rounded pale butter bow with a simple cream knot. Each is a small cloth ribbon, no animal parts and no horns.

Row 2: Four variants of a short curved tail, all attached at their lower-left end and curving up and right, left to right: (1) a short milky ivory soft C-curve ending in a tiny blush pink heart, the heart is very small relative to the tail; (2) a short fluffy cream comma tail with a tiny pale peach heart at the tip; (3) a soft ivory hook-shaped tail with a tiny lavender heart tip; (4) a short rounded white tail softly curling right, tiny pale pink heart tip. No black fill and no sharp arrow point. These must look like gentle soft tails, not wands or sticks.

Row 3: Four pairs of very small lavender wings, one mirrored pair per cell, left to right: (1) tiny rounded lavender bat-like wings with only three very soft outward scallops, no pointed tips, short narrow inner roots and no dark filling; (2) two small lilac wings with rounded outer tips, one gentle wavy lower edge and one short subtle interior curve; (3) two tiny lavender folded-petal wings with a very simple round outer edge, no inner decoration; (4) two tiny pale lavender rounded wings with short narrow inner roots and three small soft curves. Wing pairs should feel like a tiny accessory behind a cute animal, not a fantasy monster costume. Both halves must remain detached and completely inside their cell.

Row 4: Four pairs of small pale cream gossamer wings, one mirrored pair per cell, left to right: (1) each side contains two tiny rounded translucent-looking ivory teardrop lobes, one slightly larger upper lobe, one small lower lobe, one very light pastel-blue inner curve; (2) two tiny pale vanilla rounded butterfly wings with a very thin pale blue inner curve and narrow inner roots; (3) each side contains two little cream oval lobes, unpatterned, no stripes; (4) two tiny milky white wings with softly rounded outer edges and one short pale mint inner stroke. No black-yellow stripes, no bold veins, no heavy border, no extra body between paired wings.

The entire sheet must feel tiny, simple, soft, extremely cute and visually consistent with the first reference. Avoid huge costumes, armor, thorns, spikes, horns, realistic insect details, saturated colors and complex silhouettes.
