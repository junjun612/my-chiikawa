Four isolated cheek/prop placement overrides, 2026-10-20.

cheek-prop-overrides.json is the final independent proposal consumed by root.
No shared body manifest, global catalog, engine or product image was changed.

Every new center is recomputed around the existing calibrated handle point.
The source shaft fractions are the same fractions used by root's hand fit
calibration: prop04 (.30,.82), prop11 (.42,.88). Source-geometry contact point
rounding error stays below .001 body pixel. Scale .85 is applied around this
contact point. The item is tilted outward/down rather than rotating its center
in place, so the handle does not float away from the hand.

A weak 12-degree tilt/.88 scale cleared face19 but failed on the wider/lower
original cheeks. The final outward rotations are stronger to preserve both
original and new faces, while keeping the hand connected and the item readable.
All four final before/after pairs were viewed directly, then all36 expressions
on each pair were directly inspected on the four all-faces-*.jpg sheets.
The minimum visible pink cheek-to-prop gaps across all144 combinations are
5.657,5.385,5.657,4.0 logical stage pixels. These are source sprite geometry
measurements, independent of browser lighting/shadow. Root will validate actual
Canvas rendering. Detailed data is in audit.json and all-face-gaps.json.

The original files named angle-* and after-original-faces.jpg are exploratory
rejected previews. The final values are only cheek-prop-overrides.json, and the
four final all-faces-*.jpg sheets plus before-after.jpg reflect those values.
