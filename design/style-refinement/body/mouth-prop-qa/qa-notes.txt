Final broad-prop placement proposal, 2026-10-20.

Scope:26 bodies (including restored original body04) ×31 broad props ×36 faces
=29,016 face/prop combinations. Thin prop04/05/11/13/30 are excluded because
root and bodyagent own those five handle placements. Only the independent
mouth-prop-overrides.json was produced for catalog merge. No product image,
global engine, parts catalog or shared body manifest was changed by this work.

662 pair overrides:207 keep a central hold with a small downward move/shrink;
455 use an existing outer C hand, preferentially the right hand.144 pairs remain
unchanged. The other25 bodies' calculated results were preserved when body04's
31 pair records were appended. A single visual QA improvement changed the
body06 envelope from a tiny/steep right-hand candidate to a full-size envelope
with10-degree tilt in the original left hand. All31 body25 positions, including
the user-preferred prop15 [-39,-24], are unchanged.

Geometry:every facial alpha sprite is transformed at the actual body-specific
face anchor/rotation. All36 opaque face masks are combined, including colored
cheeks/tears as well as dark ink. Search uses3 logicalpx clearance; final exact
serialized-output checks require2px. Current central layouts that already clear
the36 faces are retained. Interfering layouts first try a small move below the
face or small shrink. Outer holds align a real prop alpha contour with an actual
original hand ink pixel. C-hand upper/lower ends are sampled separately to avoid
choosing a floating bbox corner. For very tall items the upper side is sometimes
held rather than forcing its lower corner upward into the face. The other hand
remains a resting hand. No arbitrary hand/prop shape was redrawn.

with2px margin; every pair retains at least6 pixels of contact with original
hand ink. Minimum changed scale is.76; no.65 shrink remains. Maximum rotation

central360px stage width. These measurements are source-sprite geometry; root

Human QA:all26 after-body_XX.jpg pages were directly viewed (all806 final pair
